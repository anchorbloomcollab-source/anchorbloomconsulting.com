About Us

Built on purpose.
Grounded in care.

Anchor and Bloom Consulting is a youth wellness and safety consulting practice founded through the partnership of two people who bring two very different and equally necessary skill sets to the same mission.

One side brings over two decades of operational leadership, safety culture, team development, and systems accountability. The other brings public health, mental wellness, community engagement, and youth-centered practice.

Together, that is Anchor and Bloom. Structure and healing. Accountability and care. Safety and growth. Not one or the other.

We work with schools, organizations, and communities across Northern California to help them build spaces where young people actually feel safe, supported, and able to grow with confidence.

What We Stand On

Our core beliefs

01

The space adults take up directly shapes the lives of young people. That is not a philosophy. It is a fact worth acting on.

02

Safety is emotional, physical, relational, and cultural. If you are only thinking about one of those, you are missing three others.

03

Healing and accountability can exist in the same room. One does not cancel the other out.

04

Youth need structure without shame and support without confusion. Adults have to know how to provide both.

05

Families and communities should be partners in the work, not afterthoughts brought in at the end of a meeting.

06

Growth happens best in spaces built with intention. Good intentions are where you start. Intentional design is where you finish.

Signature Workshop

The Space We Create

Understanding the Space We Take Up and the Space We Create for Youth

A practical session on how adults, environments, and organizational culture shape youth safety, wellness, trust, and growth.

This workshop explores how the attitudes, behaviors, systems, and energy adults bring into a space directly affect the young people in it. Drawing from public health, mental wellness, safety leadership, and lived community experience, this session helps participants reflect on the space they take up, the tone they set, and the environments they create for youth.

What Participants Leave With

Ability to identify how adult behavior and organizational culture affect youth experience.

A clearer understanding of the link between safety, wellness, trust, and growth.

Honest reflection on the physical, emotional, and relational space they create for youth.

Practical strategies to build more intentional, youth-centered environments immediately.
